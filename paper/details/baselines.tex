\section{Baseline Designs and Comparison Boundaries}
\label{app:baselines}
This section explains why a baseline's paper-native setting and its local
shared-byte variant are different experiments. The equations summarize the
interpretations documented in the repository's baseline specifications; the
original papers remain the authority for reproduction
\citep{wang2025prefixkv,tu2025vlcache,wan2024lookm}.

\subsection{Prompt Top-K and simple controls}
Prompt Top-K ranks a block by attention accumulated over a declared prompt
window, then retains the highest-ranked feasible blocks after the common
mandatory policy. Random KV uses a recorded random seed, and uniform KV divides
capacity across declared layer/head/modality strata. Value Top-K uses only the
value-novelty signal. These controls isolate whether MosaicKV benefits from
forecasting, coverage, or value information rather than merely from a different
budget or a larger mandatory suffix.

At retention one, all source K/V entries are preserved. In a shared-byte lane,
these controls use the same source cache, block granularity, and physical
packing contract as MosaicKV. Prototype merging and repair are disabled unless
explicitly part of a named control. Seeded random selection must not change
with shard scheduling order.

\subsection{PrefixKV: adaptive importance prefixes}
``Prefix'' refers to the highest-importance part of a sorted priority sequence,
not the earliest original positions. For layer $\ell$, the documented scorer
averages attention over heads and sums over prompt queries. Sorting gives
$s_{\ell,(1)}\ge\cdots\ge s_{\ell,(N)}$, with cumulative information
\begin{equation}
 P_\ell(k)=\frac{\sum_{j=1}^{k}s_{\ell,(j)}}{\sum_{j=1}^{N}s_{\ell,(j)}}.
\end{equation}
A common threshold $p$ induces the smallest $k_\ell$ satisfying
$P_\ell(k_\ell)\ge p$. Searching for $p$ allocates capacity across layers under
the global retention target. Offline profile provenance and integer rounding
are therefore part of the method, not incidental preprocessing.

The repository's interpretation shares retained positions across KV heads
within a layer, protects configured boundaries, and preserves selected K/V
unchanged. Its legacy decode eviction schedule maintains the layer-wise
allocation. Independent per-layer lengths require a compatible transport.
Padding every layer to a common length can defeat compression or invalidate
legacy mask/position assumptions.

The paper's retained ratio and the official code's forgotten ratio are
complements. Confusing them can turn mild compression into aggressive eviction.
For a shared cap, preserve the profile's relative layer shape but deterministically
apportion integer positions under the physical-byte constraint. Label this
\texttt{prefixkv\_reimpl}, byte-matched variant; it is not a newly discovered
official setting. First establish native-metric reproduction at a registered
operating point.

\subsection{VL-Cache: post-vision demand and layer sparsity}
Let $P$ be the text-query positions after the last visual token. For causal
attention matrix $A$, a row-relative filter keeps entries satisfying
$A_{qj}\ge p\max_{j'}A_{qj'}$. The paper uses $p=0.01$; this is an algorithm
parameter, not a result. Let $\gamma'_\ell$ be the head-averaged fraction of
filtered-out valid causal entries and $d_\ell=1-\gamma'_\ell$.
The documented allocation is
\begin{equation}
 \beta_\ell=\operatorname{clip}\!\left(
 \frac{d_\ell}{\sum_u d_u}\alpha L,\;0.01,\;1\right),
\end{equation}
where $\alpha$ is global retention. Token importance is
$s_j^{(\ell,h)}=\sum_{q\in P}A_{qj}^{(\ell,h)}$.
Modality awareness lies in the query boundary, not a fixed image/text-key
multiplier.

The local implementation records causal counting, clipping, integer apportionment,
recent-token protection, and query-window interpretation. Clipping need not
preserve the global sum, and nominal layer ratios need not match physical
storage after head packing. This prompt-adaptive method does not require an
offline learned profile. Disjoint calibration of ambiguous local settings is
a research control, not training claimed for the original algorithm.

In the unified lane, the physical target governs allocation. Running a
convenient paper ratio does not make its resulting cache byte-matched. The
local method remains \texttt{vl\_cache\_reimpl}; its scoring is distinct from
MosaicKV's forecast, graph, prototypes, and repair.

\subsection{LOOK-M: text prior and pivot merging}
For cumulative attention $s_j=\sum_q A_{qj}$ and text-key indices $T$, the
paper-derived text prior is
\begin{equation}
 \widetilde s_j=s_j+\mathbf1\{j\in T\}\max_u s_u.
\end{equation}
Retain a recent window and the highest-ranked remaining positions. Each evicted
key chooses a retained pivot by maximum cosine similarity within its layer/head.
The repository documents averaged, pivotal, and weighted merges. For retained
pivot $x$ and assigned members $y_1,\ldots,y_n$, the pivotal expression is
\begin{equation}
 x'=\frac{x+\sum_{i=1}^n(y_i+x)/2}{n+1}.
\end{equation}
The key-derived assignment is also used for values.

This is not MosaicKV's prototype tier: the pivot is modified, there is no
distinct residual recovery tier, and MosaicKV's modality/span filters do not
define LOOK-M pivoting. The pinned source has shortcuts differing from the
paper expression; the specification records them rather than claiming automatic
parity. A unified mandatory-token policy can also differ from the native recent
window. Enforcing it for fairness produces an explicit unified variant; the
native reproduction lane preserves the original policy.

The distinctive hypothesis is the combination of forecasted demand, relational
coverage, value contribution, and recoverable representations under an explicit
physical contract. Attention scoring, greedy selection, layer adaptation, and
KV merging have precedents. The thesis does not claim to invent them individually.
Ablations and matched-budget curves must establish whether their combination
is useful.

\subsection{Secondary baseline provenance}
H2O, StreamingLLM, PyramidKV, Elastic Cache, SnapKV, and RoCo form an optional
audited union, not an automatic list of supported model combinations. Include
a method only after pinning its repository revision, preserving its license,
verifying its cache policy, and establishing relevant metric and identity
parity. Local ports use \texttt{\_reimpl} names. More baselines do not permit
weaker sample, byte, or generation controls.
