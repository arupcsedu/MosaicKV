\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Retention and allocation.}
H$_2$O retains heavy-hitter and recent tokens; StreamingLLM protects
attention sinks with a bounded recent window
\citep{zhang2023h2o,xiao2024streaming}. SnapKV uses prompt observations to
identify useful KV entries \citep{li2024snapkv}. These works motivate
questioning whether prompt observations predict later demand, but our
study does not validate a new eviction policy. PrefixKV searches for a
layer-wise importance-prefix allocation rather than interpreting
``prefix'' as simply the first positions in sequence order
\citep{wang2025prefixkv}. We preserve this distinction and label our
profile/codec combination as an adaptation.

\paragraph{Multimodal compression.}
VL-Cache uses attention sparsity and modality-aware scoring with adaptive
layer budgets \citep{tu2025vlcache}. LOOK-M combines text-prior selection
with KV merging for multimodal long contexts \citep{wan2024lookm}.
Their published methods and native evaluations are distinct from the
single-image precision/storage ports measured here. In particular, the
ICLR compression method VL-Cache is not a similarly named recurring-image
cache-reuse system. MileBench provides long-context multimodal tasks
\citep{song2024milebench}; our current COCO study neither replaces nor
constitutes representative MileBench confirmation.

\paragraph{Quantization and latent representations.}
KIVI shows that keys and values can benefit from asymmetric quantization
axes \citep{liu2024kivi}. Our baseline precision infrastructure incorporates
axis-aware storage ideas, but no row is labeled official KIVI. Palu
decomposes projection operators, retains low-rank intermediate states and
reconstructs K/V using optimized kernels \citep{chang2024palu}.
Our representation retains the frozen model's common normalized input
with quantization, exact overlays and a lossless history path, rather than
claiming low-rank factorization or latent-cache recomputation as a new
general principle. The tested full-MHA geometry is an important restriction.

\paragraph{Serving and operator execution.}
FlashAttention reduces memory traffic through tiled attention computation,
while PagedAttention addresses allocation and sharing in serving
\citep{dao2022flashattention,kwon2023paged}. These are different boundaries
from compressing persistent representation. Our HF eager execution and
request-private CUDA replay do not establish native serving integration,
prefix-cache benefits or throughput scaling. PyTorch allocator counters
and synchronization semantics also distinguish allocated tensor memory,
reserved capacity and device timing \citep{pytorch2025cuda}; those
distinctions are preserved in the artifact and reported claims.
