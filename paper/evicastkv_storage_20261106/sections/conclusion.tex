\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented the implemented EviCastKV-S storage path: one encoded shared
prompt source, protected exact regions, position-correct reconstruction and
lossless generated history. On 256 COCO images, it uses
\EviInitial/\EviFinal~MiB initial/final storage and \EviPeak~MiB mean
audited decode-owned peak, with \EviCider{} CIDEr and
\EviMedian~s median completed-request time. The current operating point is
smaller and faster than the measured local compressed adaptations, but
does not establish quality preservation against the strongest compressed
points or byte-matched superiority. The contribution is a concrete
representation and auditable systems study, not validation of the
inactive six-component architecture. Broader claims require separately
controlled evidence rather than extrapolation from these measurements.
