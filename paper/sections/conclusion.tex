\section{Conclusion}
MosaicKV couples query-conditioned exact selection with sparse evidence
coverage, routed planning, and explicit storage semantics. Completed COCO and
MileBench studies show quality and request-time improvements over local
VL-Cache and LOOK-M ports at matched memory; Prompt Top-K remains a close
quality competitor. Native PrefixKV results improve output fidelity but show
that continuing eviction can reverse initial savings. The contribution is a
measured exact-selection system with transparent quality--memory trade-offs,
not a universal smaller-memory or serving-latency guarantee. Complete curves
and negative outcomes define the boundary for future extensions.
