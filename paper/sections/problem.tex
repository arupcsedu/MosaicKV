\section{Problem Formulation}
\label{sec:problem}
Let $\mathcal B$ be blocks from the immutable prefill cache. Each block belongs
to one layer and one KV head and carries original positions and modality
metadata. With mandatory blocks $\mathcal M$, a selected set $S$ must satisfy
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal M\subseteq S,\qquad B_{\rm init}(\operatorname{pack}(S))\le C.
 \label{eq:budget}
\end{equation}
Cost is unique live backing storage, including padding and preallocation,
rather than the selected descriptor count. At the evaluated one-token
granularity, the runtime chooses the largest feasible layout below $C$ and
records any shortfall.

An equal-memory comparison measures quality differences at identical realized
bytes. A lower-memory comparison asks whether quality is preserved at a smaller
budget. We distinguish initial, final, and peak live-cache storage at
instrumented transaction boundaries; the latter excludes weights, temporary
planner buffers, and allocator reservations. The registered 30\% frontier test
requires
\begin{equation}
 \min_{x\in\{\mathrm{init,final,peak}\}}
 \left(1-\frac{B^m_x}{B^b_x}\right)\ge0.30
 \label{eq:isoquality}
\end{equation}
and paired zero-margin quality non-inferiority. Its negative outcomes accompany
the fixed-memory results. A confidence interval containing zero does not
establish equivalence.
