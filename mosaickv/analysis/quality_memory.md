the active-memory frontier: 17 of the 32 Pareto-optimal projections are in
quality versus realized active KV bytes. Only 4 are in quality versus total
latency, while 94 of 98 MosaicKV total-latency projections are non-Pareto.

broad end-to-end latency advantage.

the exact dominating operating points instead of hiding unfavorable cases.

## Scope and limitations

- The campaign contains `532` immutable configurations and `4,256` measured
  example rows: eight paired examples for each of 14 model/task strata. This
  was the largest balanced campaign chosen for the requested execution window,
  but `n=8` per stratum is underpowered for definitive benchmark claims.
  Confidence intervals use 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples and should be
  interpreted with that small-sample limitation.
- The evaluated models are Qwen2.5-VL-3B, Qwen2.5-VL-7B, and LLaVA-1.5-7B.
  Qwen models cover MMStar, TextVQA, DocVQA, ChartQA, and VideoMME; LLaVA covers
  the four image tasks. Every run uses deterministic decoding with output
  length 128.
- Supported comparable methods are `full_kv`, `random_kv`,
  `prompt_attention_topk`, and `mosaickv_exact`; `lookm_reimpl` is additionally
  included for LLaVA-1.5. Unsupported `prefixkv_reimpl`, `vl_cache_reimpl`,
  `mosaickv_proto`, and `mosaickv_full` combinations were not converted into
  simulated or fallback rows. Their absence must not be described as a win.
- Active KV bytes, not nominal token retention, are the primary resource axis.
  LOOK-M retains its paper-faithful token granularity (block size 1), whereas
  common exact-selection methods use block size 16. This is a documented design
  difference, not an identical-granularity comparison.
- The primary quality-versus-memory rows were measured on the same A100
  backend, but an unrelated four-GPU process shared the host during parts of
  the campaign. Total- and decode-latency Pareto flags are therefore diagnostic
  rather than isolated systems claims; isolated timing claims should use the
  systems microbenchmark experiment.
- Three VideoMME retention-1 attempts were deterministic-consensus outliers on
  corrupted videos. They were rerun with the same immutable configurations,
  increased decoder EOF retries, and a high-memory Slurm allocation. Superseded
  attempts are preserved under
  `/scratch/djy8hg/runs/mosaickv/archives/main-quality-parity-fix-20260803`.
  After rerun, all 368 non-FullKV retention-1 paired rows have identical active
  bytes, token agreement 1.0, sequence agreement 1.0, logit KL 0, and
  attention-output error 0 relative to FullKV.
- The full campaign plus trace-backed validation and corrupted-video parity
  reruns exceeded the requested eight-hour target. No examples, methods, or
  failed outcomes were silently dropped to meet the wall-clock target.

## Interpretation constraints

- Score confidence intervals use paired example bootstrap against the matching FullKV answer on the same pinned sample ledger.
- Pareto flags use measured means and do not convert nominal retention into memory.
- Cache block sizes are explicit in every Parquet row: full_kv=[16], lookm_reimpl=[1], mosaickv_exact=[16], prompt_attention_topk=[16], random_kv=[16].
- Paper-faithful token methods and common block methods retain their native granularity; Pareto comparisons use realized bytes, and this granularity difference remains a disclosed design distinction.
- Diagnostic KL and attention-output error use only the fixed sample IDs embedded in every resolved configuration.
- Results with generalized or safety-fallback effective labels remain explicitly identified in Parquet and must not be described as the requested algorithm.
