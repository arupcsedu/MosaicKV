# Immutable segmented source-history batching

The prior validated consolidation reduces up to seven history projection launches
to one, but recopies growing code packets and rebuilds original-bit checks at
each seal. This variant retains all independently validated original packets and
their original phases. A device pointer table batches the identical fixed16-row
projection/RoPE operations across packets. Original correction indices are
translated into the batched destination without refitting any values.

No quantization, selection, history eviction or attention arithmetic change is
enabled. Inputs are the same two consumed engineering identities. Require mixed
raw/encoded synthetic bit parity, unchanged strided-lease sentinels, original128
model logits bitwise, logical positions and unique backing accounting. Charge
tables, duplicate correction metadata, old/new table overlap and observed
constructor temporaries. The original source packet three-bit-check constructor
is unchanged. No whole-request HBM or independent quality claim follows.

Reference is the now-validated consolidated direct source-history path, not its
slower predecessor. Initial bytes must match; metadata final and observed peak
overhead must remain <=1%. Actual increases must still be reported, not called a
compression win. CPU suite precedes two canaries; only a positive canary releases
twelve finite method/input comparisons with five warmups and twenty randomized
paired trials. At most six aggregate A100s, one GPU/12CPUs/30minutes per worker.
No fresh, calibration or paper campaign is automatically authorized.

The unsubmitted V1 plan remains immutable. V2 additionally batches the existing
three K and three V original-bit checks across layers. It does not remove any
comparison: 1,344 GPU boolean results are verified before seven atomic seals,
with seven host synchronization points instead of one per comparison. Failed
checks leave every layer's raw history unchanged and block the request. All
flags, comparison temporaries and old/new metadata overlap are charged. Include
a checks-only consolidation ablation to distinguish validation scheduling from
pointer-table batching, with the same numerical thresholds and bitwise outputs.

The actual V2 GPU canary was negative: checks-only consolidation matched all128
logits bitwise on both consumed inputs, but segmented reconstruction first
diverged at prediction33, exactly when the second packet enters batching.
The synthetic256-width controls were insufficient for the actual4096-width
model. Do not enable segmented reconstruction in the storage candidate or
promote its comparison. Preserve the negative parent artifacts for targeted
reader/projection diagnosis; no numerical tolerance has been broadened.
