K/V, original logical positions, and append-only generation.

uncertainty-guided residual repair. In particular, the master exact-selection

protocol/hardware stratum. A local port or reimplementation must be explicitly
labeled as such, with adaptation and unverified fidelity disclosed; it is not
an official upstream reproduction. No official-baseline superiority follows
from the existence of a port or its scores.
